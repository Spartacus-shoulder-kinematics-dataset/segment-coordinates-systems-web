How to read a region page

  1. Segment cards show the recommendation text, then the construction as numbered steps. Each step is a formula evaluated on landmarks detected on real bone meshes; Next draws it in 3D with the landmarks it uses highlighted. Bones switches the meshes between solid, ghost and hidden.
  2. Right / Left replays the construction on the mirrored landmarks and shows which axes must be flipped to keep the ISB orientation.
  3. Joint cards list e1, e2, e3 with the clinical meaning of positive rotations. The sliders pose the distal segment; arcs show each angle about its axis, and a warning appears near gimbal lock.
  4. Same pose in 12 sequences decomposes the current orientation with every sequence, to show how much the numbers depend on the sequence.
